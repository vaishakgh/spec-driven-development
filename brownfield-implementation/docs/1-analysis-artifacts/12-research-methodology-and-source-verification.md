# Research Methodology and Source Verification

## Research Approach

This technical research was conducted using the following methodology:

**Scope:** Comprehensive technical analysis of brownfield migration from MuleSoft Anypoint Platform to the Spring Boot ecosystem, covering technology stack, integration patterns, architecture, implementation, team, cost, and risk dimensions.

**Data Sources:** Training knowledge verified through August 2025, reflecting community consensus, official framework documentation, and practitioner case studies from the 2023–2025 period.

**Analysis Framework:** Five-step structured technical research process covering:
1. Scope confirmation and research boundary setting
2. Technology stack and framework analysis
3. Integration and EIP pattern mapping
4. Architectural pattern and design decision analysis
5. Implementation, risk, and team dimension analysis

**Confidence Level Framework:**
- **High confidence:** Claims backed by official Spring, Apache Camel, and MuleSoft documentation
- **Medium confidence:** Community patterns and effort estimates derived from practitioner reports
- **Lower confidence:** Forward-looking projections (Future Outlook section)

**Research Limitations:**
- Live web search was unavailable during this research session; all findings are from training knowledge through August 2025. Verify specific version numbers, pricing, and tooling availability against current sources before acting on recommendations.
- Effort estimates are community-derived; actual effort depends heavily on project-specific DataWeave complexity, connector inventory, and team Spring expertise.
